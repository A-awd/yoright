## Cost comparison

Reference standalone Vervotech mapping (hotel 499 USD + room 599 USD) equals approximately 4,118 SAR/month or 49,410 SAR/year, before usage, content licences, development, hosting and operations. Source: https://vervotech.com/pricing/.

Zentrum minimal SaaS indicative base alone equals 29,250 SAR/year, but is not a validated all-inclusive app cost. If a quote requires both that base and standalone Vervotech modules at published starting figures, the arithmetic becomes 6,555 SAR/month or 78,660 SAR/year, before setup/usage/UI/other extras. This is an illustrative conditional sum, not a confirmed vendor proposal, and must not be presented as a discount or final total.

Nezasa Monetise annual base is 29,340 SAR above the standalone mapping-only reference, but delivers a much broader product; that difference does not establish higher total cost than building an app independently. Nezasa Maximise is not the same scope or price as Monetise. Gimmonix costs cannot be evaluated without its percentage/base/minimum; a hypothetical 1% of 100,000 SAR monthly sales is 1,000 SAR/month, not the company's quoted fee.

SAR conversions at 3.75 SAR/USD verified at https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-us/pages/default.aspx?lang=english. Display amounts round upward. Annual arithmetic uses exact original amounts, not rounded monthly display. Taxes, banking conversion charges, supplier balances and nonpublished setup/third-party services are excluded.

## Provisional YoRight recommendation

Do not buy GIATA/Vervotech/Leonardo subscriptions before the platform route is selected; the earlier GIATA standalone suggestion applies only to a custom-build route. For a complete customer site plus future services, Juniper is the first integrated-scope proposal to evaluate, not an awarded or cheapest vendor. Zentrum Engine is a second ready-frontend commercial comparison if its final quote includes equivalent mapping/content, RTL Arabic/SAR and payment requirements. For retaining a custom YoRight frontend, Gimmonix HSP is the clearest explicit backend bundling example reviewed, with the percentage economics unresolved. Nezasa fits itinerary/tour selling, but is not the default lower-cost hotel-first option given published base and own-supplier/Arabic/SAR licensing questions.

Require one written scope across quotes: customer B2C UI versus API only, number/names of supplier connections, property and room mapping, content/image rights and Arabic, update/traffic limits, setup and recurring fees, percentage charging base, hosting, payment integration, cancellation operations and export rights. A supplier booking contract is still separate from technology connectivity unless expressly included and approved.

A ready solution is economically preferable only if setup plus recurring/usage/third-party charges plus remaining development/operations cost is lower for the same scope. Public evidence does not establish a final cheapest all-inclusive option for YoRight. Next: review comparable written offers; no vendor outreach is authorized by this research request alone.
