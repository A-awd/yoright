## تعريف مقترح للمورد

YoRight is a Saudi travel agency restarting its customer service through a WhatsApp concierge model. We currently use RateHawk and are assessing complementary hotel supply for our customers. We are seeking a retail agent booking portal first; API integration is a possible later phase after validating demand and commercial terms.

Please confirm whether you can onboard our Saudi legal entity, the required tourism licence category and documents, your non-IATA policy, available payment methods and currencies, any fees or minimums, cancellation and refund handling, and the correct regional sales contact. We will provide verified company documents through your official onboarding channel.

We will not claim historical or projected booking volumes until our records and business plan have been reviewed. A prepaid account may be considered once its settlement, refund and unused-balance terms are clear.
