# 0003 - Future sports and event ticket packages

Date: 2026-11-09 (Asia/Riyadh).
Status: accepted as an owner-directed future plan; implementation deferred.

## Decision

Record sports-match and event ticket sourcing/resale as a future YoRight product capability. Evaluate B2B suppliers, explicitly including Travel Connection and TicketNetwork, and customer packages combining a match/event ticket with flights and accommodation where commercially and operationally permitted.

The detailed backlog is [the future package plan](../docs/plans/2026-11-09-sports-and-event-ticket-packages.md). Distinguish official rights-holder distribution and hospitality from secondary-market supply, and verify authorisation for each product rather than assuming platform-wide approval.

## Consequences

- Preserve the owner's intent in the travel project's own repository.
- Evaluate final payable customer totals and full-trip cancellation exposure, not headline ticket prices alone.
- Supplier selection, terms, eligibility, prices and account access remain unverified.
- This decision does not start implementation, authorise outreach, create accounts, commit funds, buy inventory, accept contracts, or launch production sales.
- Existing product work, architecture reconciliation, security/privacy and migration merge gates remain unchanged. Easy Life remains stopped.

## Next action

Keep this item deferred until the owner resumes wholesale ticketing/packages. Then follow the staged evaluation in the linked plan, beginning with product scope and published supplier evidence.
